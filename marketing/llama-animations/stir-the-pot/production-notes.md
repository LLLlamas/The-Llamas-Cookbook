# Gather Around the Pot Animation

## Concept

Four-second 9:16 intro reel for The Llamas Cookbook. Three crowned llama mascots gently gather around a calm cooking pot, settle, and blink. This version intentionally avoids stirring, sparkles, heavy steam, fast cuts, and big character bounces.

## Local Animatic

Open `index.html` in a browser. It is a self-contained HTML/SVG animation using the existing transparent llama PNG as the character reference:

`ios-native/Resources/Assets.xcassets/Llama-Pro-Icon-Friends-Crown-Sunglasses.png`

Use it as a timing/layout preview, a social draft, or a reference storyboard for a generative video tool.

## Recommended AI Video Workflow

1. Export a first frame from this animatic at 9:16.
2. Use the group llama image as the main character/style reference.
3. Use image-to-video with a short motion-only prompt.
4. Generate 3 to 5 variants, then pick the one with the best character consistency.
5. Add final text, logo, and audio in CapCut, Canva, Runway, or another editor.

## Prompt

Use this as the starting prompt for Runway, Sora, Luma, or Veo:

```text
Create a 4 second vertical 9:16 animated mascot video. Preserve the exact cute crowned llama mascot style from the reference image: cream colored cartoon llamas, brown outlines, copper crowns, black sunglasses, white aprons, soft warm shading.

Scene: three llama mascots gently gather around a cozy cooking pot in a warm kitchen-like setting. The side llamas ease inward and settle. The center llama remains calm and friendly. The llamas blink once. The pot sits still with warm orange soup.

Motion: very simple, charming, loopable, smooth sticker-like animation. Locked camera, no major camera movement. Use only subtle easing and one blink. The llamas remain recognizable and keep their crowns, sunglasses, aprons, colors, and proportions. Keep the mood playful and premium.

Avoid: stirring, spoon movement, bubbling soup, big bouncing, sparkles, heavy steam, realistic animals, extra characters, distorted faces, missing sunglasses, missing crowns, text, watermarks, logo changes, fast cuts, scary expressions.
```

## Short Variants

- Intro: the llamas gather around the pot and the center llama stirs.
- App import: a recipe card floats in, turns into ingredients, then lands in the cookbook app.
- Grocery list: ingredients pop from the pot into a tidy checklist.
- Cook mode: the llama taps a timer and the pot bubbles on beat.
- Share: the llama slides a recipe card toward the camera with a friendly sparkle.
